STUDENT EXPERIENCE

Learning should lead to practical experience.

Health Connect Hub is building a pathway where health-related students can go beyond classroom learning by developing relevant skills and participating in professional-led projects and outreach activities.

  • Healthcare and role-relevant courses
  • Health data analysis and disease surveillance
  • Research, monitoring and evaluation
  • Health communication and education
  • Data collection and documentation
  • Digital health and project skills
  • Community mobilization and outreach
Practical exposure matters.

Students can begin building evidence of what they have learned through appropriate Health Connect Hub activities.

PROFESSIONALS & ORGANIZATIONS

Prepare for the next stage.

Professionals can create portfolios that present their qualifications, skills and experience. Organizations can create profiles describing their work, projects and workforce needs.
